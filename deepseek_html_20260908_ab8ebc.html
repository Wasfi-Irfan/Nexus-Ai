Data & Methodology

What NEXUS uses

  • Hazard information (simulated)
  • Geographical data (elevation, water bodies)
  • Environmental data (rainfall, water level)
  • Infrastructure data (roads, shelters)
  • Simulation data for demonstration

What NEXUS produces

  • Risk estimates (0–100 score with 5 levels)
  • Route recommendations (cost-based)
  • Safe-place rankings
  • Impact estimates

Prototype Risk Model

  • Hazard intensity: 35%
  • Exposure: 25%
  • Vulnerability: 20%
  • Infrastructure: 10%
  • Historical susceptibility: 10%

These are prototype assumptions.

⚠️ Important Limitation

NEXUS is a prototype decision-support system. It does not replace official emergency warnings. All simulated data is clearly labeled.
